# Code of Conduct

NEON NEXUS welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful,
patient, and constructive in issues, pull requests, and other project spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code with care; give specific, useful feedback.
- Respect different perspectives, identities, boundaries, and experience levels.
- Accept corrections and respect the maintainer's decisions and available time.
- Keep discussion relevant and protect other people's private information.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, sexual harassment, personal attacks,
doxxing, spam, and deliberate disruption are unacceptable. These expectations also
apply when someone represents the project in another public space.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns to the repository owner,
[@taggedzi](https://github.com/taggedzi). For non-sensitive concerns, open an issue
with minimal details. For sensitive concerns, use a private contact method the
owner lists on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/taggedzi), if available.
If no private contact is listed, request one without posting names, private
details, or allegations publicly. Do not use security advisories for conduct reports.

The maintainer will review reports as time allows, handle personal information
with care, and may request clarification. Absolute confidentiality cannot be
guaranteed. Depending on severity and repetition, the maintainer may remove or
edit content, issue a warning, restrict participation, or block an account.
Serious incidents may result in immediate removal. Reports made in good faith
must not lead to retaliation.

This is a solo-maintained hobby project, not a continuously monitored service.
