HOW TO PLAY

01 // MOVE

Use WASD or the left gamepad stick. Momentum is instant but smooth. Stay mobile — hostile patterns are designed to surround.

02 // AIM & FIRE

Hold LMB to aim and auto-fire with the mouse. On a gamepad, deflect the right stick or hold the fire trigger. Weapons fire for as long as you aim.

03 // DASH

Tap SPACE or the dash button. Dashes grant brief invulnerability. Charges recharge automatically.

04 // LOOT

Destroy data caches and hostile morphs for weapons, passive modules, repairs and corrupt caches. Bays are limited. Weapon pickups automatically attach while weapon bays are available; duplicates integrate into their equipped copy. When weapon bays are full, weapons wait in your reward tray, including duplicate upgrades. Passive modules always wait in the tray. Open REWARDS when ready; combat pauses while you choose. With full weapon bays and a full tray, weapons stay on the ground; a full tray also leaves modules on the ground. Protocol choices also wait for you. Ordinary equipment is paced by combat time and kills; boss loot is a bonus.

05 // SECTORS

Destroy every Null Anchor to clear a sector instantly, or survive the seven-minute timer. Sectors are endless. Difficulty escalates each jump.

06 // SYNC

Eliminating hostiles grants data XP. Every level-up offers a permanent protocol choice. Slot milestones expand your loadout. Progress auto-saves at checkpoints.
